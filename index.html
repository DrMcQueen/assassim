Field manual

How to play

Be safe and use common sense. This is just a game. We're all competitive, but it's in good fun, and nobody should get hurt, get in trouble, or have their AG ruined. 🦈

The Hunt
1
Your target When the game starts, your dossier shows one person's badge name, exactly as printed on their AG badge (bold), and full real name. Only you can see it. Track them down. You may not trade or swap targets.
2
Make the tag Get within touching distance (but do not touch anyone without their consent) and show them your screen with their exact name on it. Your target cannot dodge it: no closing eyes, no pretending not to look. Once you've got them, they open their My Code card and show their secret code. Accept your fate, no whining. 😉
3
Confirm and score The tagger types that secret code into their dossier to confirm the elimination and score one point. They then inherit the target's target and keep hunting. Points stay banked; they remain yours even after you're eventually caught.
4
Watch your back Someone out there has you as their target. Only open your code card once you've truly been tagged.
Immunity and Courtesy
On the clock. You cannot be tagged while volunteering, speaking, or working a scheduled shift for the AG or a SIG, plus 10 minutes afterward. (Example: Alice and Bob are checking badges at Hospitality, so neither can be targeted until 10 minutes after their shift ends.)
Big events. Anyone physically at or standing in line for the Gala Dinner and Misster Mensa is safe for the whole event: no tagging or being tagged. (Camping the empty ballroom afterward will not save you.)
Don't disrupt. If a tag would interrupt a speaker, performance, or organized activity, wait until it wraps up.
When and How Long
Registration closes at the end of the Gen Y Meet and Greet, 4:15 PM CST Wednesday 7/1. No one can join once the game is live.
The hunt begins at 5:15 PM CST.
The game closes Saturday 7/5 at 8:30 PM CST. Last player standing wins. If more than one player remains, the most points wins, and a tie is settled by a duel.
Staying in the Game
Keep the device you registered on with you, and log back in using the same browser you signed up with. (Registered in Chrome for iOS? Return in Chrome for iOS.) Reopened somewhere new? Use Recover my game on the check-in screen.
The app can reach you through the contact details you provided (Messenger, cell, or email) for game master matters only: emergencies like needing to drop out, a game pause for a fire drill, or reporting cheating. You will not be spammed.
Fair play: keep your target secret, never fake a hit, and show your code honestly when you're caught.
