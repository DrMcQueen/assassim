Field manual

How to play

1
The objective Outlast everyone. You start with one secret target. Eliminate them, inherit their target, and keep hunting until you're the last agent standing.
2
Your target When the game starts, your dossier shows your target's badge name (big) and full name (underneath) — visible only to you. Use them to spot your mark in the crowd by matching their badge. No one else can see who you're hunting.
3
Make the tag Find your target and tag them using your group's agreed method (your game master explains it at kickoff). Once tagged, they open their My Code card and show you their secret code word.
4
Confirm the hit Type the secret code they showed you into your dossier and submit. Only their exact code works, so there's no faking a kill from across the room.
5
Inherit and continue The instant you confirm, your eliminated target's mark becomes your new target. Keep going without pause.
6
Watch your back Someone out there has you as their target. Your secret code is your life — only reveal it (via the My Code button) when you've genuinely been tagged.
7
Win When the chain loops all the way back and you're the only agent left in play, the operation closes and the victory is yours.
Eliminated? In the live game you're out for good — stay and watch the leaderboard. In a practice round, you may be able to rejoin.
Fair play: don't reveal your code unless you're truly tagged, and never fake a hit.
