Field manual

How to play

Be safe and use common sense. This is just a game. We're all competitive, but it's in good fun, and nobody should get hurt, get in trouble, or have their WeeM experience ruined. 🦈

The Hunt
1
Your target When the game starts, your dossier shows one person's badge name, exactly as printed on their event badge (bold), and full real name. Only you can see it. Track them down. You may not trade or swap targets.
2
Make the tag Get within touching distance (but do not touch anyone without their consent) and show them your screen with their exact name on it. Once a safe, valid tag has been made, acknowledge it honestly. Then they open their My Code card and show their secret code. Accept your fate, no whining. 😉
3
Confirm and score The tagger types that secret code into their dossier to confirm the elimination and score one point. For a primary-target tag, they inherit the next eligible target. An outside open-contract claim keeps the claimant’s primary target. Points stay banked; they remain yours even after you're eventually caught.
4
Watch your back Someone out there has you as their target. Only open your code card once you've truly been tagged.
Immunity and Courtesy
Meet-and-greets and good judgment. The GM cannot monitor or referee what happens at every meet-and-greet. Use good judgment when tagging each other: respect consent, personal space, and the people around you. Do not chase, grab, block anyone’s path, or interrupt an activity to make a tag. If a tag would be unsafe, disruptive, or make someone uncomfortable, wait. Handle uncertainty calmly and bring unresolved disputes to the GM afterward.
On the clock. You cannot be tagged while volunteering, speaking, or working a scheduled shift for the event, plus 10 minutes afterward. (Example: Alice and Bob are checking badges at Hospitality, so neither can be targeted until 10 minutes after their shift ends.)
Announced safe periods. Follow any additional no-tag times or locations announced by the GM.
Don't disrupt. If a tag would interrupt a speaker, performance, or organized activity, wait until it wraps up.

Activity Check-ins
When check-ins are required, scan the current QR and open its link before your personal deadline. Wait for Check-in recorded. The GM sets the QR period and check-in extension before the game starts; the default is 12 hours. Each accepted scan or GM credit starts one configured interval from server acceptance. Only one credit per player per QR period is allowed; repeats do not add time. Timers run overnight. Recovery, opening the app, and tagging do not count.
The GM may credit an eligible player for a legitimate issue, with a recorded reason, before the deadline. This is labeled a GM credit, not a camera scan. Credits cannot revive a permanently eliminated player. Missing a required deadline is permanent; points remain recorded.
The GM may turn required check-ins off. While off, there are no inactivity eliminations, but tags, resurrections, and the final cutoff continue. Re-enabling gives active and resurrecting players a fresh configured interval, capped by game close. Accepted period credits stay used. The shortened final period must be checked in or credited while requirements are on. Every game closes October 31 at 11:00 PM Central.
Open Contracts and One Resurrection
After 12 hours of actual play, all active survivors become open contracts. You keep one primary target and may also claim anyone on the open board. An outside contract earns one point while you keep your primary target. Every hunter assigned to the victim receives an eligible replacement. The game starts with normal target inheritance; revenge assignments can create shared targets after players return.
Your first elimination by another player automatically starts one resurrection: a one-hour cooldown from the server-recorded tag. During cooldown you cannot hunt or be targeted, but must keep checking in while the GM requires it. Existing QR scans and deadlines stay unchanged. You may decline resurrection and permanently leave the game.
After the hour, if play continues, you return with a fresh secret code and immediately become an open contract. Your original hunter becomes your primary target. If unavailable, follow their elimination chain to an active fallback. When your original hunter returns, your primary target switches back. Shared targets are allowed, and all affected hunters receive private assignment alerts. Your next tag is permanent. Inactivity and GM removal never trigger resurrection.
A sole active hunter wins immediately, even while other players are resurrecting. Pending returns stop when the game ends. At the final cutoff, only active hunters qualify, with returned and never-eliminated hunters treated equally; most points wins, with a GM-settled tie.
Revenge and GM Chaos
Tag your original hunter after returning to earn the Revenge achievement once per game, with the normal one tag point. Fallback targets do not qualify. Your original hunter stays fixed by your first confirmed player-tag elimination. Counter-revenge is allowed, but each player still has only one resurrection.
The GM may trigger a Chaos Shuffle for active players, including returned players. Disable Chaos restores the normal revenge rules and earlier ordinary targets where still valid; it does not undo tags or returns that already happened. A full shuffle can replace revenge primary assignments; the GM can instead protect current revenge pursuits. Original-hunter history, achievements, scores, check-in deadlines, open contracts, and cooldown timers remain unchanged. A later original-hunter return restores the revenge assignment. Players still resurrecting receive their normal revenge assignment when they return.
Game Awards
Top Hunter goes to the highest score among active hunters, including returned hunters. Only if no active hunter remains are eliminated players considered. The GM settles a tie. First One Out goes to the first valid player-tag victim, even if they later resurrect. QR timeouts and GM removals do not qualify.
Staying in the Game
Keep the device you registered on with you, and log back in using the same browser you signed up with. (Registered in Chrome for iOS? Return in Chrome for iOS.) Reopened somewhere new? Use Recover my game on the check-in screen.
The GM may use the contact details you provided (Messenger, cell, or email) for game master matters only: emergencies like needing to drop out, a game pause for a fire drill, or reporting cheating. You will not be spammed.
Fair play: keep your target secret, never fake a hit, and show your code honestly when you're caught.
