How to play

1
The objective Outlast everyone. You start with one secret target. Eliminate them, inherit their target, and keep hunting until you're the last agent standing.
2
Your target When the game starts, your dossier shows one person's badge designation — visible only to you. That's your mark. Find them in the crowd. No one else can see who you're hunting.
3
Make the tag Tag your target using your group's agreed method (your game master will explain it at kickoff). Once you've tagged them, they hand over their badge name to you.
4
Confirm the hit Type your target's badge name into your dossier and submit. Only the exact name works, so there's no faking a kill from across the room.
5
Inherit and continue The instant you confirm, your eliminated target's mark becomes your new target. Keep going without pause.
6
Watch your back Someone out there has you as their target. Your badge name is your life — only reveal it when you've genuinely been tagged.
7
Win When the chain loops all the way back and you're the only agent left in play, the operation closes and the victory is yours.
Eliminated? In the live game you're out for good — stay and watch the leaderboard. In a practice round, you may be able to rejoin.
Fair play: keep your target secret, never fake a hit, and hand over your badge name honestly when you're caught.
